GOOD TO KNOW

The little
details.

A few lovely details before you place your bouquet request.

Can I choose specific flowers for my arrangement?

You choose between one and three flower color families; we choose the specific shades, flowers, and greenery. Our arrangements are designer's choice, which allows us to select fresh, beautiful blooms based on the season and your palette. Ramo Buchón bouquets are made with roses only.

How far in advance do I need to order?

At least 21 calendar days (three weeks). The order form automatically blocks earlier dates so we have time to source your flowers and make your arrangement with care.

Is the vase included?

Yes. Medium and large floral arrangements come styled in a round glass vase with greenery. Ramo Buchón bouquets are wrapped arrangements with no vase or greenery.

Do arrangements include ribbons?

Medium and large vase arrangements do not include ribbon. Ramo Buchón bouquets come wrapped and finished with a ribbon chosen by EuroFleur Studio; ribbon colors and styles cannot be requested. Some example photos may show ribbons as styling inspiration.

Do you deliver?

Not yet! We currently offer scheduled local pickup in Canal Winchester, Ohio. The exact pickup address is provided only after your order is personally confirmed. Delivery may become available later.

Does submitting a request mean my order is confirmed?

Not quite. Your preferred pickup date and time are requests until we personally confirm them. No payment is collected on the website. After you submit, we'll follow up from justfloralmoments@gmail.com.
