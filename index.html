GOOD TO KNOW

The little
details.

A few lovely details before you place your bouquet request.

What is a EuroFleur Signature Arrangement?

Our $175 Signature Arrangement is a full, romantic, European-inspired mix of seasonal flowers and complementary greenery, designed in a round glass vase. You choose your preferred color families, and we select the blooms and create the finished design. Every arrangement is unique.

What is a Ramo Buchón bouquet?

A Ramo Buchón is a bold, tightly gathered bouquet made entirely of roses, wrapped for a dramatic presentation. Choose 50, 75, or 100 roses. It includes no greenery or vase and is finished with a ribbon selected by EuroFleur Studio. Pricing varies by rose count, starting at $175.

What's the difference between a Signature Arrangement and a Ramo Buchón?

Choose the Signature Arrangement for a garden-inspired mix of seasonal flowers, textures, and greenery already arranged in a glass vase. Choose the Ramo Buchón for a statement-making wrapped bouquet of roses only. Both are made to order and available for pickup.

What is a last-minute arrangement?

If you need a Signature Arrangement with less than 21 days' notice, call or text 814-769-6244 before placing a request. Last-minute arrangements cost $200 and depend on flower and pickup availability; they are not guaranteed.

How much do the arrangements cost?

The EuroFleur Signature Arrangement is $175. Ramo Buchón bouquets start at $175, with the final price depending on whether you select 50, 75, or 100 roses. A last-minute Signature Arrangement is $200 when available. Applicable tax may be additional.

Can I choose the colors?

Yes! For a Signature Arrangement, select one to three color families. We'll choose the individual flower varieties and shades to create a cohesive, seasonal design. For a Ramo Buchón, the rose color options are shown in the order form; availability may vary.

Will my bouquet look exactly like the photos?

Our photos show the overall floral style and mood, not a promise of identical flowers or an exact finished arrangement. Signature Arrangements vary with the season and available blooms. Your selected color palette guides the design.

How do I schedule pickup?

Select an available pickup date and 15-minute time slot in the order form. Pickup is in Canal Winchester, Ohio, between 8 a.m. and 9 p.m., subject to availability. Your selection is a request until we confirm it personally.

What if my preferred pickup date is unavailable?

Please choose another available date. If you need flowers within the standard three-week notice period, text or call 814-769-6244 to ask about a last-minute Signature Arrangement. Some dates may be blocked entirely.

How do I pay?

Payment is arranged after your bouquet request is personally confirmed. The website does not currently collect payment at checkout. Contact us if you need to discuss payment options before ordering.

Can I choose specific flowers for my arrangement?

You choose between one and three flower color families; we choose the specific shades, flowers, and greenery. Our arrangements are designer's choice, which allows us to select fresh, beautiful blooms based on the season and your palette. Ramo Buchón bouquets are made with roses only.

How far in advance do I need to order?

At least 21 calendar days (three weeks). The order form automatically blocks earlier dates so we have time to source your flowers and make your arrangement with care.

Is the vase included?

Yes. Our signature floral arrangement comes styled in a round glass vase with greenery. Ramo Buchón bouquets are wrapped arrangements with no vase or greenery.

Do arrangements include ribbons?

Our vase arrangements do not include ribbon. Ramo Buchón bouquets come wrapped and finished with a ribbon chosen by EuroFleur Studio; ribbon colors and styles cannot be requested. Some example photos may show ribbons as styling inspiration.

Do you deliver?

Not yet! We currently offer scheduled local pickup in Canal Winchester, Ohio. The exact pickup address is provided only after your order is personally confirmed. Delivery may become available later.

Does submitting a request mean my order is confirmed?

Not quite. Your preferred pickup date and time are requests until we personally confirm them. No payment is collected on the website. After you submit, we'll email you from justfloralmoments@gmail.com.
