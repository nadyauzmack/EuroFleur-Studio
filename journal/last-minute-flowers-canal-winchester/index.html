Ordering flowers · EuroFleur Studio

Last-Minute Bouquet Requests in Canal Winchester, Ohio

Need flowers in less than three weeks? Use the Rush Request calendar in our online form. Rush fulfillment is not guaranteed.

Published October 19, 2026 · Canal Winchester, Ohio

What is a rush bouquet request?

A rush request is for a preferred pickup date within the next 21 days. EuroFleur Studio's standard made-to-order bouquets require at least 21 calendar days' notice, but our online order form now has a separate Rush Request calendar for shorter-notice dates. All flowers are pickup only in Canal Winchester, Ohio.

How to submit a rush request online

Visit our bouquet order form, choose your arrangement and preferred color families, then select Rush Request. Choose a date in the rush calendar and a preferred 15-minute pickup time, enter your contact details, and submit the form directly. There is no need to open your email app or call to submit.

How much does a rush bouquet cost?

A $25 rush surcharge applies to every rush request. A EuroFleur Signature Arrangement normally costs $175, so a rush Signature Arrangement is $200. A Ramo Buchón rush request also has $25 added to the normal price of the selected 50-, 75- or 100-rose bouquet. Applicable tax may be additional. Payment is only arranged after approval.

Is a rush request guaranteed?

No. Rush fulfillment is not guaranteed. Flower availability, existing orders, and pickup capacity may prevent us from accepting a short-notice request, even if you can select the date. Dates blocked on the calendar cannot be requested. Sending a form does not reserve flowers or guarantee a pickup slot.

When will I hear back?

Every order request—standard or rush—will receive an approval or denial by email within 48 hours of submission. Watch the email address you provided and check your spam folder if necessary. If approved, we will send pickup and payment details. Please do not send payment until your request is approved.

What if my date is unavailable?

Try another available date in the rush calendar or choose a standard date at least 21 days away. If you have a question, you can also call 814-769-6244, but the online form is the main way to submit requests.

Planning ahead for future gifts

For birthdays, anniversaries and celebrations, standard notice gives us more time to plan. Browse our flower collection, read the pickup guide, and use the online order form to request your date.

Flowers made just for you

EuroFleur Studio creates romantic, designer-choice arrangements for scheduled pickup in Canal Winchester, Ohio. Standard requests are for pickup dates at least 21 days away. Rush requests within 21 days have a $25 surcharge and are not guaranteed. Choose either calendar in our online order form; we email an approval or denial within 48 hours. Pickup only in Canal Winchester.
