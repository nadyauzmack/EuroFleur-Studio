Flower Pickup in Canal Winchester: How Ordering Works

EuroFleur Studio works a little differently from a traditional walk-in flower shop: we design made-to-order bouquets for scheduled pickup in Canal Winchester, Ohio.

Published October 19, 2026 · Canal Winchester, Ohio

Step one: Choose your flower style

Our Signature Arrangement is a romantic mix of seasonal flowers and greenery arranged in a round glass vase. Our Ramo Buchón is a wrapped, roses-only statement bouquet in your selected rose count. Both are prepared to order rather than pulled from a walk-in display.

Step two: Select your palette

For a Signature Arrangement, choose one to three color families. We select the individual varieties and exact shades based on what is fresh and available. This designer-choice approach means your flowers will have their own natural personality, even if you use a photo for inspiration.

Step three: Request your pickup date and time

Use the online order form to choose an available date and 15-minute pickup slot. The available window is generally 8 a.m. to 9 p.m. daily, subject to blocked dates and the studio's confirmation. Standard orders require at least 21 days' advance notice. Selecting a date or submitting a request does not by itself guarantee an order.

If a date is blocked, select a different day. Need flowers sooner? Call or text 814-769-6244 to ask about a last-minute Signature Arrangement, which is $200 when available.

Step four: Wait for personal confirmation

We confirm order details, pickup availability, and payment arrangements directly with you. Online checkout is not currently used to collect payment. The exact Canal Winchester pickup address is shared privately after confirmation; it is not published on the website.

What if you are driving from Columbus?

Customers from Columbus, Groveport, Reynoldsburg, and Pickerington are welcome to request pickup, but all flowers must be collected in Canal Winchester. Plan to transport your flowers upright and keep them away from heat inside the car. For a complete overview, see our pickup areas page and flower ordering FAQs.
