How to Choose the Right Bouquet Style and Size

A bouquet's impact is about more than its size. Shape, flower type and the way the arrangement will be displayed all affect how it feels.

Published October 19, 2026 · Canal Winchester, Ohio

First, choose your presentation

Would the recipient enjoy mixed seasonal blooms arranged in a vase, or a dramatic bundle of roses wrapped to make a statement? Our EuroFleur Signature Arrangement is a single $175 vase arrangement with greenery and multiple floral textures. We no longer offer separate medium and large sizes for this style.

Signature Arrangement: one thoughtful size

The Signature is designed to feel full, romantic and garden-inspired. Flower varieties change with the season, and you can choose up to three color families. A glass vase is included, so there is no need to find a separate container before displaying the flowers.

Ramo Buchón: choose your rose count

For a wrapped, roses-only bouquet, we offer Ramo Buchón options with 50, 75 or 100 roses. A 50-rose bouquet is already a statement gift; 75 and 100 roses create an increasingly dramatic presentation. These arrangements do not include a vase or greenery. The studio chooses the finishing ribbon.

Match the bouquet to the moment

A vase arrangement is convenient for a host gift, a birthday or a thank-you. A wrapped rose bouquet can create an especially memorable first impression at a romantic celebration or milestone. Think about what the recipient will do with the flowers after opening the gift, too.

What if you need flowers soon?

Standard requests require 21 days' notice and scheduled pickup in Canal Winchester. For dates within 21 days, select Rush Request through our online form. Rush adds $25 ($200 for a Signature Arrangement), fulfillment is not guaranteed, and an approval or denial is emailed within 48 hours.

One size does not mean one identical design

Our Signature Arrangement has one pricing tier, but every bouquet is made with a different combination of seasonal flowers. The mix of larger blooms, lighter textures and greenery varies naturally. If you have previously seen multiple size options on the website, the current collection has been simplified to one $175 Signature style.

The choice is meant to be straightforward: choose a romantic mixed-flower arrangement in a vase, or choose a rose count for a wrapped Ramo Buchón. There is no need to estimate stem counts for the Signature Arrangement or pick individual varieties.

How space and transport affect the choice

Think about where the flowers will live. A vase arrangement is designed to sit upright on a table, while a wrapped bouquet may need a suitable vase and careful arrangement after it is unwrapped. If you are bringing flowers to someone at an event, consider how they will transport the gift home.

Do not leave flowers sitting in a hot vehicle. If you need to drive from Columbus, Reynoldsburg or another nearby city, build the pickup journey into your day. Read the pickup guide for practical tips and remember that all appointments must be confirmed directly.
