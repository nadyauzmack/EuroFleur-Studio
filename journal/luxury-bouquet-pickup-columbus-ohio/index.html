Romantic Bouquet Pickup Near Columbus, Ohio

Looking for something a little more intentional than an off-the-shelf bunch of flowers? A made-to-order floral arrangement can be a beautiful choice for a milestone, a special gift, or an ordinary day worth celebrating.

Published October 19, 2026 · Canal Winchester, Ohio

Romantic flowers made with intention

Our floral style takes inspiration from European garden arrangements: gentle movement, a considered color palette, natural texture, and a touch of vintage romance. The goal is not perfect symmetry. It is a bouquet that feels generous, softly composed, and personal.

EuroFleur Studio's Signature Arrangement is $175 and includes a round glass vase. Flower varieties and exact shades are selected by the studio within your chosen color families. Each design is one of a kind, depending on what flowers are in season.

Prefer all roses?

For a dramatic, roses-only gift, explore a Ramo Buchón bouquet. These wrapped arrangements come in 50, 75, or 100 roses, without greenery or a vase. Their structured shape creates a different visual effect from the more relaxed Signature Arrangement.

Nearby Columbus, but pickup is in Canal Winchester

Although people searching for Columbus flowers may discover EuroFleur Studio, we are based in Canal Winchester and provide pickup there only. We do not deliver to Columbus or other towns. Customers in Groveport, Pickerington, Reynoldsburg, Gahanna, or the greater Columbus area can request a pickup appointment if the journey works for them.

Pickup timing is requested in the online form. Orders generally need 21 calendar days of notice, and the exact address is sent after confirmation. Find more logistical information on the areas served page.

Make an occasion feel considered

Think about whether the flowers should feel airy or bold, the recipient's favorite palette, and how they will display them. A glass vase can make gifting easier; a wrapped rose bouquet makes an immediate statement.
